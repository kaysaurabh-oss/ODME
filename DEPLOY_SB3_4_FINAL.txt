Final behaviour
---------------
- One concise market-language narrative: market action, entry logic, path/hurdles, next action and what to watch.
- Feed timestamps are shown in SGT.
- Internal indicator names are not used in the trader-facing narrative.
- TV-only instruments get a subtle note that no options-positioning layer is configured.
- Fresh ODME is integrated into directional trades, path/ETA, hurdles, strike selection and non-arrival/theta decisions.
- SuperBrain automatically checks, when ODME is available, for:
  * directional futures long/short
  * Short CE
  * Short PE
  * Short strangle / range non-arrival theta
- A directional setup may be entered as late-but-still-valid when the cleaner trigger was slightly earlier but the present location/path is still acceptable. Otherwise SuperBrain gives the preferred pullback/re-entry condition rather than chasing.
- When a trade is issued the narrative explicitly says: "SuperBrain is taking this exposure now."
- That exposure is written as one TRADE row and later scans manage the same row as HOLD / ADD / REDUCE / EXIT.
- Broker positions are never read.
- Options are model exposures by strike + underlying spot basis; exact option premium is retained when available but is not required for the visible trade sentence.
- No background shadow trading occurs between scans. Exposure begins only when a scan actually issues a trade.

Non-arrival theta
-----------------
- Bearish positioning may produce a Short CE when the current ODME side-action says CE selling is acceptable and price/momentum are not actively accelerating toward the strike.
- Bullish positioning may produce a Short PE on the mirrored logic.
- Range-bound theta may produce a Short Strangle when the options map favors containment and directional pressure/momentum are balanced.
- Expansion/trap conditions block fresh theta and can reduce/exit existing theta exposure.
