What changed
1. SuperBrain user control is now "Scan now". No Ask-AI/Ask-SuperBrain handoff or build/model/version label is shown in the Level-3 terminal.
2. A manual scan refreshes ODME/options whenever the instrument has a saved expiry, even when batch scan is disabled.
3. Freshness is shown first: scan timestamp, EDGE/AURORA/STRUCTURE/LIQUIDITY timestamps and ODME timestamp/expiry.
4. No ACTIVE campaign:
   - current indicator state
   - nearest relevant location / hidden-intelligence line
   - next likely event / pending Level-2 setup
   - explicit ENTRY READY or HOLD against the stored ODME requirement
   - compact ODME verdict/action plus POC, CE/PE walls, safer CE/PE and value area when available
5. ACTIVE campaign:
   - campaign / exposure
   - actual behaviour now versus expected behaviour
   - next locked management event being watched
   - current ODME bias + precise options question
   - next likely NEW campaign/event and what must happen
   - compact ODME section
6. Manual scans are observational for ACTIVE campaigns. They do not automatically close, roll, add, reduce or otherwise mutate a campaign.
7. Fresh entries still become ACTIVE only after the user confirms "Trade taken — record campaign".
