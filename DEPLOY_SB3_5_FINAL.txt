Liquidity intelligence
----------------------
- The narrative identifies the most recent liquidity RUN or SWEEP/reclaim event when present.
- It states the nearest unfinished liquidity above and below price and interprets them in context.
- For a fresh directional short, unfinished liquidity above price is treated as an adverse entry threat when it lies inside the proposed trade's risk area; SuperBrain waits for it to clear or be swept/reclaimed before entering.
- For a fresh directional long, the mirrored rule applies to unfinished liquidity below price.
- Liquidity in the intended direction is treated as a useful target magnet; it can become the first objective even when it is a single nearby pool.
- A directional ADD is suppressed while adverse unfinished liquidity remains inside the risk area.
- For Short CE / Short PE, a proposed sold strike is rejected if unfinished adverse-side liquidity reaches or extends beyond that strike.

Trade and exposure behaviour
----------------------------
- The scan-driven architecture is unchanged: SuperBrain does not invent historical fills or background trades between scans.
- It can still classify an entry as late-but-still-valid when the cleaner trigger was slightly earlier but current entry quality remains acceptable.
- When a trade is issued, the narrative clearly says: "SuperBrain is taking this exposure now" and the current scan spot is the recorded entry reference.
- The same TRADE row is then managed on later scans rather than creating repeated exposures.

Follow-on management wording
----------------------------
- HOLD: clearly states the existing exposure and original spot, why the thesis remains intact, whether a new user may still enter now, and what would weaken/exit it.
- ADD: clearly states that SuperBrain is adding now, the current spot, and the pullback/momentum logic permitting the add.
- REDUCE: clearly states that SuperBrain is reducing the existing exposure and why conviction has weakened without necessarily invalidating the thesis.
- EXIT: clearly states that SuperBrain is exiting now and why; no automatic re-entry is assumed.
- Directional exposures state the invalidation and only permit additions on a qualified pullback with aligned pressure/momentum.
- Short CE / Short PE management explicitly describes protection, expansion-risk deterioration and sold-strike arrival conditions for HOLD / REDUCE / EXIT.
- Short strangle management explicitly requires range containment to remain dominant and exits on expansion/protection failure.
